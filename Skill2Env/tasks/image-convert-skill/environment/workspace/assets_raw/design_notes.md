# Design handoff notes — 2023-04-18 (M. Riedel, brand team)

Quick notes for whoever wires these up on the site:

- We export everything as **PNG at quality 100** — please keep it that way,
  the brand team does not like compression artifacts.
- The hero should be displayed at **1800px wide**.
- Logo goes on the white header, so transparency should not matter.
- No formal size budget yet; just keep things "reasonably small".

Raw exports are in this folder as they came out of the tools (`hero_banner.tiff`
and `press_release_photo.tiff` are straight out of the press pipeline, hence
CMYK). `campaign_mockup.psd` is the campaign working file, do not publish.
`legacy_hero_2021.png` is from the 2021 campaign, kept for reference.
