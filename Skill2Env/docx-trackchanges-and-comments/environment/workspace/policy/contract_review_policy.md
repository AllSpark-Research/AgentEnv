# Bluepeak Analytics, Inc.
# Commercial Contract Review Standards

**Document:** Legal-Std-014, Revision 2025.1
**Effective:** January 15, 2025
**Owner:** Office of the General Counsel

These standards govern the review and markup of inbound vendor paper. Deviations
require Legal approval as noted per rule.

---

## Rule 3 — Payment Terms

Bluepeak's standard payment terms are **Net 30**: invoices are payable no later
than thirty (30) days after receipt. Do not accept payment terms longer than
thirty (30) days without Finance approval.

## Rule 4 — Auto-Renewal

For agreements with automatic renewal, Bluepeak must have the ability to
terminate at the renewal boundary with not less than **sixty (60) days'** written
notice before the end of the then-current term. Vendor-drafted notice windows
shorter than sixty days must be revised.

## Rule 5 — Limitation of Liability

Unless the General Counsel approves otherwise in writing, the aggregate
liability cap must be **no less than twelve (12) times the monthly subscription
fee** payable under the agreement. Compute the required cap from the fee
schedule in the agreement itself, not from estimates.

## Rule 6 — Governing Law

Bluepeak's required governing law for vendor agreements is the **State of
Delaware**. Vendor-drafted governing-law clauses selecting any other state must
be revised to Delaware.

## Rule 7 — Security Incident Notification

Bluepeak's standard requires vendors to notify Bluepeak of confirmed security
incidents affecting Bluepeak data within **forty-eight (48) hours** of discovery.
Redlining a vendor's breach-notification clause requires **prior written
approval from the General Counsel**; absent that approval, raise the issue as a
reviewer comment only.
